# OnRecord identity

OnRecord was renamed from Your Spotify; it remains a fork of [Yooooomi/your_spotify](https://github.com/Yooooomi/your_spotify).

The vinyl-record mark and wordmark were generated with Codex for this fork. They are not Spotify branding and do not imply affiliation.

- `wordmark.png`: full composition, ink-on-transparent, used where a horizontal lockup fits.
- `symbol.png`: the record mark alone, used as a CSS mask so it inherits each theme's text color.
- `social.png`: wordmark on an ivory background for link previews.
- `../logo192.png`, `../logo512.png`, `../favicon.ico`: app icons derived from the symbol, white ink on a dark field.

Generation prompt: “Design a logo for OnRecord, a self-hosted app that records Spotify listening history and lets you explore it. Abstract vinyl-record inspired mark: a minimal record disc with grooves and a center spindle, subtly suggesting a recorded play log, paired with a clean "OnRecord" wordmark. Monochrome solid black on white, flat vector style, simple enough to work in one color as a small icon, sophisticated and understated, professional and cool, not trying too hard. Must not resemble Spotify's logo or use Spotify green. Produce one final image.”

An earlier listening-history mark from the Your Spotify fork era is preserved in git history.
